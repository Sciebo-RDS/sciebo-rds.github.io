For Institutions

We know managing research data across departments, faculties, and entire institutions is a complex challenge. bridgit provides a flexible, ready-to-integrate, open-access solution for research data management in cloud infrastructures.

bridgit is built to strengthen the entire research ecosystem Whether you manage a university cloud, run a library service, or handle sensitive data outside academia, bridgit gives your institution a flexible foundation for transparent, FAIR, and secure research data management.

For Universities and Academic Institutions​

bridgit integrates directly into your existing university cloud (such as sciebo) and turns it into a full-featured research data connection hub to external data repositories.

Your advantages:

  • Offer an in-house, GDPR-compliant RDM solution for all disciplines
  • Reduce administrative overhead by connecting existing tools and repositories
  • Enable seamless data sharing, archiving, and publication from one interface
  • Strengthen your institution’s commitment to Open Science and FAIR principles bridgit makes research data management a built-in service of your university and doesn’t add an extra burden for researchers within their research data workflow.

For Service Entities (e.g. Libraries)​

Services, such as libraries, play a key role in guiding researchers through the data lifecycle. bridgit makes that process easier, smarter, and more consistent across departments.

Your advantages:

  • Centralize data management plans and project documentation
  • Simplify metadata curation and annotation with flexible templates
  • Manage and archive large annotated datasets
  • Monitor compliance with institutional and funding requirements With bridgit, support teams can focus on building capacity without chasing file versions.

For Non-Academic Institutions​

Whether you handle biological samples, satellite data, or industrial research results, bridgit adapts to your workflows while ensuring security and traceability.

Your advantages:

  • Configurate and manage (sensitive) datasets securely within your own infrastructure
  • Collaborate with academic and public partners on shared projects
  • Maintain full control over access, storage, and data lineage
  • Integrate your existing tools via open connectors and APIs bridgit literally bridges the gap between research, innovation, and industry collaboration, tailored to your needs: safely and efficiently.
